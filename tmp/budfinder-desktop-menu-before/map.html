How search matches work

An exact strain name uses listings for that name only. A partial name can match several strains. Shops are counted once even when they list several pack sizes.

The map starts around your selected city; other cities remain available through Show all cities. Location filters narrow results separately. Counts exclude unavailable and incorrectly attributed menus.

More about matching and coverage
How price comparisons work

Map averages use the lowest listed price per shop for the same strain, currency and unit, across at least two shops nationwide. Menu value averages the percentage differences for the strains that can be compared.

Menu dates appear on shop results. Growers, batches, origins and pack sizes may differ. These are listed-price signals, not quality ratings or live stock. Open a menu for its source and a detailed price comparison.
